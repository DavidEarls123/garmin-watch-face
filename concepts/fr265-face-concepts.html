Ten faces for a runner, golfer, pilot and United fan

Concept mock-ups drawn at the watch's real resolution. Each one lists the data it shows and whether that data comes from the watch itself or from the internet through your phone. All numbers are sample values for Wednesday 25 November, and the fixture shown is made up.

Beyond the standard data

A watch face can fetch from the internet through a background task. It runs at most every 5 minutes, via the Garmin Connect app on your phone. Here's what's realistic for your interests.

ItemPossible?How
Flying
METAR for your airfield, decodedYesaviationweather.gov has a free JSON API with no key. Covers Irish stations (EIDW, EICK, EINN, EIKN, EIME and others). You set the ICAO code in the phone settings. Small strips without a METAR use the nearest station.
Flight category colour (VFR / MVFR / IFR / LIFR)YesComes with the METAR, or is worked out from ceiling and visibility.
Crosswind and headwind for your runwayYesCalculated on the watch from METAR wind and a runway heading you enter.
Density altitudeYesFrom METAR temperature, QNH and field elevation.
Sunset and legal night (Ireland/EASA: end of evening civil twilight, sun 6° below the horizon)YesCalculated on the watch. No internet needed.

Fun extras

Small touches that can go on any of the layouts.

Matchday takeover

From two hours before kick-off, any layout switches to the Matchday face. It switches back at full time.

Fergie time

After 90 minutes the live clock shows stoppage time as 90+3, 90+4…

Result tint

The accent colour glows red the day after a win and goes grey after a loss. It can be turned off.

Go / no-go light

A small green or red dot shows whether the weather is within your own limits: category, wind and crosswind you set.

Daylight left

A countdown to sunset, which suits golf and VFR flying. It turns amber in the last hour.

Heartbeat seconds

The seconds ring pulses at your actual heart rate while you're looking at the watch.

Tap-through slots

Tapping a slot opens the related Garmin glance, e.g. HR, Body Battery or weather.

Colour themes

Pick presets from the phone: United red, Neon (like your current face), Cockpit amber, Fairway green.

Moon phase

Calculated on the watch, which helps when planning a night flight.

How live data reaches the watch

This is how the METAR and the football scores arrive.

Public APIaviationweather.gov, football-data.org
→
Small proxy (recommended)A free Cloudflare Worker. It hides your API key and shrinks the replies to a few bytes so they fit in the watch's memory.
→
PhoneThe Garmin Connect app relays the request over Bluetooth.
→
Face background taskRuns every 5 minutes or more and saves the result for the face to draw.
  • Without your phone nearby, the face shows the last data it got, with its age (e.g. "METAR 47 min old").
  • Watch faces have tight memory limits, so each fetch should return just the few values needed.
  • The always-on mode on AMOLED has to stay mostly black, so every layout above has a simpler sleep version.
