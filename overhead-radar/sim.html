Case

Screen: Waveshare 5″, 1080 × 1080, with the Pi's kiosk scale set to 2.25× (as setup-pi.sh does). The layout is the same 480-point grid you see here.

Settings you change on the screen are kept in this browser, as they would be on the Pi. Power cycle restarts the software; Clear saved settings returns everything to the defaults.
